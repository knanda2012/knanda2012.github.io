04 / ABOUT ME

A little about me.

I'm Krishnananda C P, a doctoral researcher in Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, India.

My research interests lie at the intersection of acoustofluidics, soft matter, particle dynamics, and nanobioscience. I enjoy moving between experiments and computational models to understand the mechanisms behind complex behavior.

Outside the details of any single project, I am motivated by the broader question of how simple physical interactions give rise to striking collective phenomena.
